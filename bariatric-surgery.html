Bariatric Surgery

Gastric sleeve, gastric bypass and metabolic weight-loss procedures, performed by experienced surgeons at accredited hospitals.

Gastric Sleeve

Laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy for sustainable, long-term weight loss.

Gastric Bypass

Roux-en-Y bypass for patients with severe obesity or metabolic conditions.

Revision Surgery

Corrective procedures for patients who have had a prior bariatric surgery.

What's Included

01

Pre-Op Assessment

02

Surgery & Hospital Stay
