Frequently Asked Questions

How does medical tourism work? +

You share your medical reports, we identify suitable specialists and hospitals, you receive treatment and cost options, and we coordinate travel, admission and follow-up.

How do I choose the right hospital? +

Your coordinator matches your condition to hospitals with the relevant specialty programme, then presents the options with their differences explained.

How much does treatment cost? +

Cost depends on the procedure, hospital, room category and length of stay. We provide an estimated range first, then an itemised quote from the hospital.

Can you help with medical visas? +

Yes. We provide the hospital invitation documents required for a medical visa and guide you through the application steps.

How long do I need to stay? +

This varies by procedure. Your treatment plan will state the expected hospital stay and the recommended recovery period before flying home.

Can I speak with the doctor before travelling? +

Yes. A video consultation with the treating specialist can be arranged before you commit to travel.

Can I upload my medical reports? +

Yes, through the form on this page. Reports help the medical team give a more accurate assessment.

What happens after treatment? +

You receive discharge documents, a follow-up schedule and a point of contact for questions after you return home.

Do you provide airport pickup? +

Yes. Airport transfers, local transport and accommodation near the hospital are part of the coordination service.

How do I get a treatment estimate? +

Submit the consultation form with your reports and preferred treatment. Estimates are ranges, not guaranteed prices.
